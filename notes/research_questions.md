# Research questions

## Primary question
Can adaptive ray selection recover hidden internal structure in a sealed,
heterogeneous heritage object using fewer projection measurements than uniform
or random sparse tomography?

## V0 hypotheses
1. Adaptive acquisition will improve hidden-feature recovery at low ray budgets.
2. Adaptive acquisition will reduce reconstruction error faster per acquired ray.
3. Any advantage should be visible before adding learned reconstruction methods.

## Important limitation
This repository is a synthetic proof-of-concept. The phantom, attenuation model,
noise model, and adaptive policy are intentionally simple and are not yet a
validated model of a particular archaeological material or scanner.
